PHONOLOGY

Compare sound patterns

Minimal pair

In English, swapping [s] for [t] changes see to tea. Two words that differ by one sound in the same position can show a phonemic contrast.

Aspiration

In many English varieties, /p/ has a puff of air at the start of stressed pin. After [s] in spin, the puff is much smaller. These are allophones of /p/.

Where sounds occur

English typically permits [ŋ] at the end of sing. Tagalog also permits it at the start of words, as in ngipin [ŋipin], meaning “teeth.” Each language has its own sound patterns.
